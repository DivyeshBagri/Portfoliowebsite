IWhy we exist

Most brands don't have a design problem. They have a story problem. They know what they sell, but not why anyone should care. We start by listening, dig until we find the one true thing, and build everything around it: the name, the colours, the words and the launch. When the story is clear, the design almost writes itself.

MK Meera Kulkarni
Founder & Creative Director

IVHow we work

Four steps from first call to launch day

A typical identity project takes 10 to 14 weeks. You will always know which step we are on and what comes next.

  2. 01 · Weeks 1–2

    Listen

    We start with workshops and customer interviews. No sketches, no moodboards, just questions until we understand what makes you different.

    • Founder workshop
    • Customer interviews
    • Audit
  3. 02 · Weeks 3–4

    Discover

    We turn what we heard into a brand story: one page that says who you are for, what you believe and why it matters. You sign this off before any design starts.

    • Brand story
    • Positioning
    • Naming
  4. 03 · Weeks 5–10

    Create

    Design and words develop together. We show two or three strong directions, refine one with you, then build it into a full system.

    • Identity
    • Copy
    • Packaging
    • Web
  5. 04 · Weeks 11–14

    Launch

    We produce the launch assets, train your team on the guidelines and stay on for the first campaign so the story arrives in the world exactly as planned.

    • Guidelines
    • Launch campaign
    • Team training
