The studio

Storytellers who happen to design

We started in 2014 above a print shop in Pune with two desks, one borrowed computer and a belief that good brands are told, not decorated.

Twelve years later we are a team of 22 strategists, designers, writers and makers. We have worked with first-time founders, family businesses in their third generation and companies listed on the stock exchange.

What hasn't changed is the order we work in. Story first, then everything else. We would rather spend an extra week finding the right idea than an extra month polishing the wrong one.

Our story so far

Twelve years in five chapters

2014

Two desks above a print shop

Meera and Arjun leave their agency jobs and take on their first client, a local bakery that still uses the logo today.

2017

The first national launch

A tea brand's packaging goes into 2,000 stores. The team grows to nine and moves into a real studio.

2020

Learning to work apart

We go remote, start a content practice and help forty small businesses move online in a single year.

2023

Motion and digital join the family

Kabir's motion studio merges with ours. Websites and product design become a third of our work.

2026

Twenty-two people, one table

We open our new home in Koregaon Park, still above a print shop, now with our own risograph.

What we believe

Three rules that decide how we spend our time and yours.

Story before style

We never open a design file until the brand story is written and agreed. Pretty is easy; true is hard.

Small teams, senior hands

Every project is led by a director who stays with you from the first call to launch day. No hand-offs to juniors.

Measured, not guessed

We agree on what success looks like before we start, and we report on it after launch. Beauty that doesn't work isn't finished.
